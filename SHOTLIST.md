# SHOTLIST — AleqFactory, one-day shoot brief

Purpose: replace every art-directed placeholder on the site with photographs of **this plant**.
No stock, no AI renders. Treatment in post: slightly cool blacks, warm highlights on the tower.
Every published photo gets a mono caption with place and time (« Centrale, 05:48 — premier
chargement »), so **record the time of each shot**.

## Timing

One day, two golden windows. Arrive **before 05:00** (plant lights still on, first trucks
queueing); stay through **dusk** (tower against the last light — the hero frame).
